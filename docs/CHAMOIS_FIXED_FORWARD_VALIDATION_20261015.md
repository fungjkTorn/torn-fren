The historical median-cycle-path candidate improved Chamois arrival stock availability from 69.57% to 71.97% with 100% departure coverage and a shorter mean wait. Its latest historical day was worse. This justifies a separate subsequent validation, not a model replacement.

No other item, model or threshold can replace this primary comparison after results are inspected. As in the established replay, the 288 five-minute starts are after the left boundary through the right boundary. Both primary models update causally during the window; no parameter tuning, new feature, threshold or item selection is allowed. The source models are unchanged from the previous numerical source; the new helper adds protocol validation only. Adaptive-selector and residual-correction diagnostics are not primary comparisons and their window initialization is not evidence for either primary model.

This is predeclared forward-period reconstruction from subsequent sampled stock history, not actual live issuance, flights, purchases or a blinded lockbox. All five-minute starts pooled are primary; UTC hourly, all twelve UTC-minute offsets, raw success/failure/UNKNOWN/PENDING/ABSTAIN counts, coverage, wait, matched outcomes and chronological slices stay visible. These cohorts are highly correlated and must not be treated as 288 independent trials.

For a useful follow-up lead, require at least 200 known outcomes per primary model, at least 90% departure coverage, no more than two percentage points coverage loss, no higher average wait, more matched wins than losses, at least two percentage points higher resolved success, and no more than two percentage points additional UNKNOWN mass. Report every metric even if the screen fails. One 24-hour period cannot establish long-term consistency; no automatic deployment or promotion follows any result.
